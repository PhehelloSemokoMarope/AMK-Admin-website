Admin Portal

The AMK Clothing Admin Portal is a comprehensive, web-based Fashion Design Management System tailored to streamline daily boutique operations, custom garment production, and storefront management. 

Designed specifically for staff and administrators, this centralized dashboard empowers the team to efficiently manage the entire customer lifecycle—from initial order creation and bespoke measurement recording to final payment processing and invoicing. 

Key capabilities include real-time inventory tracking with automated low-stock alerts, dynamic storefront product management, dedicated client-tailor chat support, and detailed reporting modules for sales and customer orders. 

Built with a responsive, modern interface and integrated directly with a secure Firebase backend, the admin portal ensures that staff have immediate, organized access to critical business data, enabling seamless coordination between front-of-house customer service and back-of-house tailoring operations.
